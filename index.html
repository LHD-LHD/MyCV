Hongda Li 李洪达

Technical Director, NIMO Labs

I received my Ph.D. and B.Eng. in Engineering from Tsinghua University.

I work at the intersection of neural engineering, computational modeling, and artificial intelligence. My research focuses on epidural electrical stimulation (EES), personalized neuromodulation, and data-driven methods for restoring motor function after spinal cord injury.

My work spans mechanistic simulation, neural activation prediction, electrode design, multimodal movement assessment, and clinical translation. I have participated in five EES clinical trials and served as a primary clinical lead in three of them, including the first such trial in China.

2020–2026National Engineering Research Center for Neuromodulation

Digital Spinal Cord Project — Clinical Translation

  • Participated in five EES clinical trials and served as a primary clinical lead for three, including the first trial in China; all five participants recovered lower-limb functions including standing and walking.
  • Designed a clinically deployed 32-contact spinal electrode and optimized its geometry and stimulation configurations, improving stimulation performance by 20%.
  • Developed a multimodal movement-assessment framework integrating EMG, motion capture, EEG, heart rate, and biomechanics to quantify motor recovery and support adaptive rehabilitation.
2021–2026Ph.D. Research · Tsinghua University

Versatile Neural Activation Predictor

  • Developed a structure-aware deep-learning predictor for the biophysical MRG axon model, jointly encoding the extracellular-potential profile, fiber diameter, axon length, and stimulation pulse width.
  • Enabled activation-threshold prediction across diverse axon geometries and neuromodulation scenarios with 96.9% accuracy and over 1,000-fold computational acceleration relative to iterative biophysical simulation.
  • Integrated the predictor with electric-field models to support rapid electrode evaluation, neural-target analysis, and personalized stimulation design.
2021–2026Ph.D. Research · Tsinghua University

Patient-Specific EES Modeling & Closed-Loop Optimization

  • Built personalized digital spinal-cord twins coupling three-dimensional finite-element electric fields with dorsal-root and dorsal-column activation across nine fiber diameters.
  • Constructed bilateral stimulation–response datasets containing over 12,000 samples, with neural features spanning 20 anatomical regions paired with 16-channel EMG; trained patient- and side-specific five-fold ensemble models for muscle-response prediction.
  • Developed an end-to-end pipeline linking Bayesian parameter recommendation, FEM simulation, neural activation, EMG prediction, and five movement objectives.
  • Created a complementary Coordinate-GCN workflow that directly predicts multi-channel muscle responses and ranks 1,000 candidate settings per round for accelerated parameter recommendation.
2023COMAC Flight Test Center

Flight Data Analysis & Visualization
